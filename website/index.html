— Our Story

Born from
art, not fashion

Romualda was born in 2020, named after Romualda — an eccentric Dutch artist who became an unforgettable figure in the childhood of sisters Mariana and Cristina. She was a woman who dressed as if every day were a performance, a living canvas moving through the world.

Today, Cristina hand-paints every piece in their Madrid workshop, using Moroccan egg pigments, natural oils, and unique blends of experimental inks. Each garment begins as a blank possibility and ends as something no one else will ever own.

"We don't design clothes. We paint them." — Cristina, Creative Director
Visit Our Atelier

— The Process

From Brush to Body

Every piece begins with a blank canvas and a loaded brush.

01

Natural Pigments

Cristina sources Moroccan egg pigments, natural oils, and experimental inks from artisans across Europe and North Africa.

02

Painted by Hand

Each garment is stretched over a frame in the Madrid atelier. Cristina then paints directly onto the fabric, layer by layer.

03

Set & Cured

Natural pigments are set using traditional techniques — some requiring days of drying in natural light to achieve the right depth of colour.

04

Sealed & Signed

Every piece is finished with a handwritten certificate of uniqueness and sealed with our signature wax stamp before shipping.
